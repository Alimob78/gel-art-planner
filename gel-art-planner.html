Gel and preparation

For 1% the band positions come from the measured curve of a real run (the fastest-band setting is ignored) and are scaled by run time, taking the photographed run as 10 min. Run time scales how far every band travels. The default run is 10 min for 1% and 2% and 15 min for 4%, where the fastest band reaches the position above. Position and lane pitch (0.75 cm) and the 7.3 cm run length (about 9.7 lane pitches) come from a photo of a real E-Gel EX, so lanes are shown at true proportions. Position is assumed geometry for the prediction. Check them against a pilot run.
